# Naming

## Recommendation: keep **Flight Record**

It does the job you wanted:

- **Flight** covers space and shooters.
- **Record** covers vinyl.
- Bonus double meaning: a *flight recorder* (black box) logs a flight, and here the record *is* the flight log. A "record" is also a high score.
- Short, easy to say, easy to turn into a logo (a ship silhouette over a spinning disc).

Possible tagline: *"Every side is a sortie."*

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Angle | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run-Out Groove | The end-of-side groove that ends your level | Clever for vinyl people, less obvious to others |
| Needle Drop | Starting a record; also an audio term | Strong vinyl identity, weak shooter identity |
| Side A | Each side is a level | Very short, hard to search for |
| Wax Wing | Vinyl ("wax") plus flight | Playful, slightly Icarus-flavored |
| Groove Squadron | Squad/co-op feel | Fits multiplayer; a little generic |
| B-Side Blitz | Arcade energy | Reads as more casual than the concept |
| Spin Shot | Spinning disc plus shooting | Simple; might read as a sports or billiards title |
| Lock Groove | A record trick with stuck loops, doubles as "lock on" | Obscure |

## Before committing

- [ ] Search app stores, itch.io, Steam, and general web for existing games or products named Flight Record / Flight Recorder.
- [ ] Check trademark databases in your region.
- [ ] Check that the domain, GitHub org/repo name, and a social handle are available.
- [ ] Say it out loud with the typical phrasing ("Have you played Flight Record?") to make sure it works in conversation.

## Repo naming

Suggested repo name: `flight-record`. The working title can change later without affecting the architecture; keep the name out of code identifiers where practical (use a neutral namespace such as `Game` or `Core`) to make a rename cheap.
