Spawn positions are randomized per-run. Counts, spreads, and entity types are Inspector-configured (baked) on each Sector prefab.
How Sectors Work
Main.unity no longer holds a baked Sector instance — a SectorSpawner component instantiates a tier prefab in Awake(), weighted-picked from SectorTypeTable.asset's Tiers array for the current GameControl.CurrentSector: Sector L0 for the player's first sector, Sector L1 from sector 2 through 4, Sector Triceptor LT at sector 5 specifically, then an even Sector L1/Sector L2/Sector L3 0.333/0.333/0.333 weighted pick from sector 6 onward. Because the assignment is data on the asset rather than hardcoded thresholds, tiers can also be configured to compete probabilistically within an overlapping sector range instead of switching outright — sector 6+ is itself an example of this now. Instantiate() does not apply scene-instance Inspector overrides, so each tier's config (size, entity counts, spreads, completion %, portal reference) is baked directly into its own prefab rather than overridden per scene.
A Sector is a Unity prefab with a Sector MonoBehaviour. Play area size is set by the _size Inspector field (world units) — the SpriteRenderer that previously drove this has been removed and the Sector's transform scale is now (1, 1, 1). On Start(), the component spawns each entry in its List<EntitySpawn> at a random position within that entry's spread zone, offset from the Sector's own world position. All spawned objects become children of the Sector transform.
Entity types are configured as a List<EntitySpawn> — each entry carries a Prefab, Count, and Spread. Any number of types can be added or removed in the Inspector without code changes. Spread is a 1–100 value: 100 fills the full sector area; 90 constrains to 90% of each axis from center; 50 clusters to the inner half. Spread shapes a square region, not a circle.
halfSize = _size / 2
range = (spread / 100) × halfSize
posX = sector.x + Random(−range, +range)
posY = sector.y + Random(−range, +range)
Sector L1 uses _size = 500, giving a half-size of 250 units. A spread of 90 produces a ±225-unit spawn window on each axis. The area was deliberately widened so the player has to explore rather than seeing the whole enemy population from spawn. Sector L0 uses _size = 150 (half-size 75 units, ±67.5-unit spawn window at 90% spread) — a smaller, faster-to-clear opening sector for new runs. Sector L2 and Sector L3 both keep the same _size = 500 as L1 but raise the entity composition instead of widening the play area further — L2 adds Probe II and Triceptor as live enemy types plus more asteroids, while L3 leans into a heavy Satellite IV presence (×20) backed by lighter Probe/Triceptor counts.
Sector Types
A sector's type (objective) is layered orthogonally on top of its tier (L0/L1 above) rather than baked into the tier prefab. Assets/Config/SectorTypeTable.asset weighted-picks a SectorType per sector number; SectorSpawner applies it to the freshly instantiated Sector prefab before that Sector's own Start() runs.
Type Sector Range Weight Win Condition
Clear Hostiles 1 1.0 80% of spawned enemies destroyed
Clear Hostiles 2-4 0.5 80% of spawned enemies destroyed
Relic 2-4 0.5 Collect the relic dropped by its seeded carrier
Large Threat 5 1.0 Destroy the boss + its escort (kill-all, not %)
Clear Hostiles 6+ 0.5 80% of spawned enemies destroyed
Relic 6+ 0.5 Collect the relic dropped by its seeded carrier
Relic seeds one already-spawned enemy or asteroid (picked at random, preferring enemies) as the carrier. Destroying it drops a Relic pickup (glyph *) with its own health — collecting it opens the portal, but shooting it down instead fails the sector (same Game Over flow as player death, distinct "RELIC LOST" subtitle).
Large Threat is exclusive to sector 5, where it always pairs with the dedicated Sector Triceptor LT.prefab tier (see Tiers in SectorTypeTable.asset) — Clear Hostiles/Relic aren't eligible for sector 5. It has its own win-condition override: Sector carries a second _largeThreatEntities list (the boss + escort) separate from the base _entities list, and IsCleared/ObjectiveFraction require that second list fully destroyed rather than a percentage — the base list (Asteroids, for this prefab) is optional flavor/cover, not part of the objective.

Single tough enemy in place of the usual crowd — Triceptor LT.prefab shares Triceptor.prefab's glyph (f) and 3-cannon volley AI but at MaxHealth: 750 (vs. Triceptor's much lower base health) and 3× visual scale, with a thin Probe II escort rather than a full enemy roster.
